Courses

Math 495

Math 495 - Models in Mathematical Biology

This is the primary course to take if you are interested in learning about mathematical biology.

Under Professor Cooney, this course will cover the following topics: Discrete and Continuous Models, Nondimentionalization, One-Dimentional ODE models for Population Dynamics, Stability Analysis and Bifurcation Models for Infectious Disesase, Replicator Equations, Equilibria and Stability for Systems of Nonlinear ODEs, Lotka-Voltera, Lypanov Fuctions, SIR Models, Evolutionary Game Theory, PDEs with Space-Time Ecological Dynamics, Reaction-Diffusion Equations, Turing Instabilites, and Spatial Pattern Formation
