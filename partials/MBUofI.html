What is Mathematical Biology?

Mathematical Biology in the simpilest sense is the study of biological structures through mathematical models. In this broad subject, research is conducted from a variety of topic such as optimal vaccine dispersion for COVID-19 (1), the effect of a vaccine scares on childhood vaccine percentages (2), and spatial pattern formation with biologically derived PDEs (3).

Mathematical Biology Faculty at the University of Illinois

At UIUC, many professors both in and out of Mathematics could be classified as Mathematical Biologists, but, for the sake of brevity, the ones in the math department are Professors DeVille, Rapti, Kirpatrick, Cooney, Yu, and Clifton. Some of the many professors outside of Mathematics are Professor O'Dwyer in Statistics, and Professor Maslov in Physics.
